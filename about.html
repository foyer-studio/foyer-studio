Process

The Approach

I spend a few minutes walking the property before I shoot anything. That walkthrough tells me which rooms need attention, which angles to avoid, and where the light is best. Most photographers skip this and it shows.

Post-processing is calibrated to accuracy, not saturation. The goal is a photograph that sells — one that makes a buyer want to walk through the door, not one that makes them feel deceived when they do.

Origin

Why Foyer Studio Exists

I started Foyer Studio because most listing photography around here felt like an afterthought. Blown-out windows, cluttered frames, images shot from the doorway and called done. Buyers make decisions from online photos now — the first showing happens on a screen.

The name comes from the foyer — the first room a buyer walks into. That moment at the threshold, deciding whether to step further in, is exactly what these photos are trying to recreate.

“The work is only as good as the attention it receives. I do not scale by hiring. I scale by caring more.”

— Abhi Bhullar, Foyer Studio
